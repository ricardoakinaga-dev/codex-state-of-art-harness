## Closure states

Each residual high-risk branch must end in exactly one of:
`TESTED_PASS`, `TESTED_FAIL_FIXED`, `UNREACHABLE_PROVEN`,
`DEAD_CODE_REMOVED`, `BLOCKED_ENVIRONMENT`,
`DEFERRED_BLOCKING_PROMOTION`, or
`NOT_HIGH_RISK_RECLASSIFIED_WITH_EVIDENCE`.

## Stop conditions

Any residual branch that can plausibly cause authority bypass, filesystem
escape, data corruption, incorrect recovery/terminal status, stale acceptance,
false PASS, silent unsafe retry, or unsafe migration remains a promotion
blocker until directly proven safe. No broad coverage pragma, exclusion,
renaming, or classification downgrade may close it.
